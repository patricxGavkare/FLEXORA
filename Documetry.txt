Phase 1: DEAM & Simulation Enhancements (FLEXORA)

1. MediaPipe Real-Time Computer Vision Pipeline:
   - WebAssembly (WASM) + GPU-accelerated HandLandmarker model.
   - Tracks 21 3D hand keypoints live through standard webcam.

2. Continuous Proportional Flexion Kinematics:
   - Replaced binary on/off snapping with scale-invariant continuous ratio (0.0 to 1.0).
   - Bionic prosthetic joints now articulate continuously (0° to 42°) in real time.

3. Logic & Truth Table Collision Fixes:
   - Pointing mapped to 10111 (Index straight, others bent).
   - Thumbs Up mapped to 01111 (Thumb straight, others bent).
   - Peace Sign mapped to 10011 (Index & Middle straight).
   - Precision Pinch mapped to 00111.
   - Resolved binary key collisions in handLogic.js.

4. Embedded MCU & PWM Servo Actuator Controller (McuPanel.jsx):
   - Designed for the DEAM (Digital Electronics and Microprocessor) course rubric.
   - 5-Channel Servo PWM telemetry (1000 µs - 2000 µs at 50 Hz).
   - 8-bit GPIO Data Register (PORTB) display with binary, hex, and decimal representations.
   - Simulated UART serial telemetry string ($FLEX,...) for hardware link.
   - Dynamic current draw estimation (mA) based on active motor load.

5. Client-Side Machine Learning Studio (mlClassifier.js & AiTrainerPanel.jsx):
   - 66-dimensional scale and translation-invariant normalized feature vectors.
   - Few-shot custom grip training with on-device Euclidean centroids.
   - Softmax temperature-scaled probabilistic confidence meter (0-100%).
   - Model weights persistence (localStorage) and JSON export capability.

Phase 2: Next-Level Apple-Grade Frontend & DEAM Expansion (FLEXORA)

6. Surface Electromyography (sEMG) Oscilloscope (SemgPanel.jsx):
   - Simulates biological muscle electrical signals (microvolts µV) from forearm Flexor and Extensor muscle groups.
   - Real-time 60 FPS dual-channel oscilloscope trace.
   - Live DSP metrics: RMS (Root Mean Square in µV), MAV (Mean Absolute Value), and SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio in dB).
   - Switchable digital signal filters: Raw Bipolar, Full-Wave Rectified, and Low-Pass Linear Envelope.

7. Closed-Loop Tactile Force & Haptic Grasp Lab (HapticGraspPanel.jsx):
   - Addresses the core challenge of bionic prosthetics: not crushing fragile payloads.
   - 5 virtual test objects: Fresh Egg, Aluminum Soda Can, Paper Cup, Brass Key, and Ripe Strawberry.
   - Force-Sensing Resistor (FSR) voltage divider calculation (0 to 5.0 V).
   - 10-Bit ADC register simulation: 0x000 to 0x3FF (0 to 1023) mapped to ATmega328P ADC0..ADC4.
   - Microcontroller firmware safety: "MCU Auto-Crush Protector" interrupt routine that detects when force reaches 88% of rupture limit and automatically clamps motor PWM.

8. Kinematics Panel & Manual Slider Override Mode (KinematicsPanel.jsx):
   - Displays continuous MCP (0°-90°), PIP (0°-100°), and DIP (0°-70°) joint articulation angles for all 5 digits.
   - Features "Manual Hardware Override Mode" with 5 Apple-style sliders (0% to 100% flexion) so the prosthetic hand can be demonstrated and evaluated even without a camera!
   - 8 one-click clinical grip presets (Power Fist, Open Hand, Precision Pinch, Pointing, Peace Sign, Thumbs Up, Lateral Key, Hook Grip).
   - 5-bit control bus register display with decimal, hex, and active truth table decoder.

9. Unified 5-Instrument HandTracker Workstation (HandTracker.jsx):
   - Integrated 5-tab segmented control switcher:
     Tab 1: Kinematics & Grips
     Tab 2: DEAM Microcontroller Bus
     Tab 3: AI Model Calibrator
     Tab 4: sEMG Muscle Oscilloscope
     Tab 5: Tactile & Haptic Lab
   - Camera overlay controls: Toggle Wireframe Skeleton, Toggle Landmark Nodes, Freeze Stream.
   - Seamless fallback and state propagation between manual sliders and live vision.

10. Apple-Grade Website Redesign (Home.jsx & Home.css):
    - Interactive 5-stage System Architecture flowchart with clickable nodes for deep circuit and mathematical theory inspection.
    - Complete DEAM Curriculum Integration Matrix covering Unit 1 (Logic Gates & Number Systems), Unit 2 (Combinational/Sequential Circuits & Latching), and Unit 3 (Timers, Fast PWM, and SAR ADC).
    - Quantitative Engineering Benchmarks Table comparing FLEXORA with Ottobock Michelangelo and Open Bionics Hero Arm.
    - Interactive Technical FAQ Accordion explaining core engineering principles in simple English.
    - Clean, authentic minimalist Apple aesthetic with SF Pro typography, frosted glass navbar, and zero clutter.
